Privacy policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Wagdoku is made by Fidovasoft. You can play the whole game offline. We do not create accounts and we keep no personal data on our own servers: your name, chosen dog, levels, streak, treats and settings are stored only on this device and are deleted when you uninstall the app or use Reset progress.

Ads: when your device is online, the free version shows ads from Google AdMob between some levels and when you choose to watch a video for a reward. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address and ad interaction data to show, limit and measure ads and, where you allow it, to personalize them. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in Settings. On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases are handled by the store you got the game from. We never see your payment details.

If you send us feedback by email, we receive only what you choose to write, and we use it only to reply and to improve the game.

Questions: fidovasoft@gmail.com
